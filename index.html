Our services

Comprehensive dental services for every smile

At All Smiles Dental Care, we offer a complete range of dental treatments to meet your needs at every stage of your oral health journey—from routine check-ups to advanced dental care, everything your smile needs, all under one roof.

Smile Makeover

Your dream smile, beautifully designed.

Transform your smile and boost your confidence with a personalized smile makeover. Our comprehensive approach combines advanced cosmetic dentistry with modern dental technology to enhance the color, shape, alignment and overall appearance of your smile.

Smile makeover treatment options

  • Teeth Whitening – Brighten and refresh your smile by reducing stains and discoloration.
  • Dental Veneers – Enhance the shape, size, color and appearance of teeth with thin, customized veneers.
  • Dental Bonding – Correct minor chips, gaps and shape irregularities with tooth-colored composite material.
  • Dental Crowns – Restore damaged or heavily worn teeth while improving their appearance and function.
  • Clear Aligners – Improve the alignment of teeth with a discreet, removable orthodontic option.
  • Gum Contouring – Refine the gum line to create a more balanced and harmonious smile.
  • Dental Implants – Replace missing teeth with natural-looking, functional tooth replacements.
  • Composite or Ceramic Restorations – Restore damaged teeth while maintaining a natural-looking aesthetic.

Every smile is unique. Our dental team carefully evaluates your oral health, facial features and smile goals to recommend the treatments best suited to you.

A personalized approach to a naturally beautiful smile.

Dental Implants

Replace missing teeth. Restore your smile. Reclaim your confidence.

Restore your smile, confidence and ability to eat comfortably with dental implants—a long-term tooth replacement solution designed to closely replicate the look, feel and function of natural teeth.

We provide personalized implant treatment planning based on your oral health, bone condition and individual needs. From implant placement to the final customized crown or restoration, our team focuses on precision, comfort, aesthetics and long-term function—whether you are replacing a single missing tooth or multiple teeth.

Dental implant procedures

  • Single Tooth Implant – A reliable solution for replacing one missing tooth with an implant-supported crown.
  • Multiple Dental Implants – Strategically placed implants that support multiple replacement teeth and restore function and aesthetics.
  • Full-Mouth Implant Rehabilitation – A comprehensive solution for patients who have lost most or all of their teeth.
  • Implant-Supported Bridges – Stable support for replacing several adjacent missing teeth without relying on neighboring natural teeth.
  • Implant-Supported Dentures – Secure and comfortable dentures supported by implants for improved stability while eating and speaking.
  • Immediate Implant Placement – In suitable cases, an implant may be placed at the time of tooth extraction, depending on bone and gum conditions.
  • Bone Grafting & Sinus Lift – Additional procedures may be recommended when there is insufficient bone to support an implant.

Our implant treatment process

Consultation & Evaluation Digital Planning Implant Placement Healing & Integration Final Crown / Prosthesis Follow-Up Care

Every case is carefully evaluated to determine the safest and most appropriate treatment approach for achieving a functional, natural-looking smile.

Advanced planning. Precise treatment. Confident smiles.

Braces & Clear Aligner Treatments

Straighten your teeth. Improve your bite. Smile with confidence.

Achieve a straighter, healthier and more confident smile with personalized orthodontic treatment. We offer a range of braces and clear aligner options designed to suit different dental needs, lifestyles and aesthetic preferences.

Treatment options

  • Clear Aligners – A discreet, removable option for gradually correcting teeth alignment and selected bite concerns.
  • Metal Braces – A time-tested and effective option for treating a wide range of orthodontic problems.
  • Ceramic Braces – Tooth-colored or clear brackets that provide a more discreet alternative to traditional metal braces.
  • Self-Ligating Braces – Modern braces designed with specialized clips instead of traditional elastic ligatures.
  • Retainers – Essential after orthodontic treatment to help maintain your new tooth position and protect your results.

Our orthodontic team evaluates your teeth, bite and individual goals before recommending the most suitable treatment option.

Root Canal Treatment

Save your natural tooth. Relieve pain. Restore your smile.

Root canal treatment is a procedure designed to treat infection or inflammation inside a tooth while preserving the natural tooth structure. We use modern techniques and advanced technology to make root canal treatment precise, comfortable and efficient.

Our treatment may include

  • Single-Visit Root Canal Treatment – Efficient treatment for suitable cases, reducing the number of appointments.
  • Multi-Visit Root Canal Treatment – Recommended when additional infection control or complex treatment is required.
  • Retreatment – Treatment of a previously root-treated tooth when infection or symptoms recur.
  • Post & Core Restoration – Helps restore structurally weakened teeth after root canal treatment when clinically indicated.
  • Crown After Root Canal Treatment – A customized crown may be recommended to protect and strengthen a significantly weakened tooth.

Our team carefully evaluates each tooth and recommends the most appropriate treatment based on your dental condition and long-term oral health.

Modern techniques. Gentle care. Natural teeth preserved.

Pediatric Dentistry

Gentle dental care for growing smiles

We provide child-friendly dental care designed to protect healthy teeth, prevent dental problems and build positive dental habits from an early age. We focus on creating a comfortable, friendly environment where children can feel relaxed and confident during their dental visits.

Pediatric dental treatment options

  • Children's Dental Check-Ups – Regular examinations to monitor oral development and identify problems early.
  • Preventive Dental Care – Professional cleaning, fluoride application and personalized oral-hygiene guidance.
  • Dental Sealants – Protective coatings that help prevent cavities in the grooves of permanent teeth.
  • Fluoride Treatment – Helps strengthen tooth enamel and reduce the risk of tooth decay.
  • Cavity Treatment – Gentle restorative care for children's decayed or damaged teeth.
  • Pulp Therapy – Treatment to preserve baby teeth affected by deep decay or infection.
  • Space Maintainers – Used when appropriate to help preserve space for developing permanent teeth after early tooth loss.
  • Habit & Oral Development Guidance – Advice regarding habits such as thumb sucking, pacifier use and proper oral development.
  • Early Orthodontic Assessment – Monitoring jaw and tooth development to identify potential alignment concerns early.

Our goal is to make every visit comfortable, positive and educational, while helping children develop healthy smiles that last a lifetime.

Healthy habits. Happy visits. Growing smiles.

Laser Dentistry

Advanced technology for more comfortable dental care

We use modern laser technology in selected dental procedures to provide precise, minimally invasive treatment where clinically appropriate. Laser dentistry can help improve treatment precision while supporting a comfortable patient experience.

Laser dentistry applications

  • Gum Treatments – Laser technology can be used in selected procedures to treat and reshape gum tissue.
  • Gum Disease Management – Helps support the treatment of certain periodontal conditions as part of a comprehensive treatment plan.
  • Soft-Tissue Procedures – Useful for selected procedures involving the soft tissues of the mouth.
  • Frenectomy – Laser-assisted treatment may be used to release restrictive frenum tissue when indicated.
  • Gum Contouring – Helps refine the gum line for improved smile aesthetics in suitable cases.
  • Oral Lesion Management – Certain soft-tissue lesions may be treated or managed using dental lasers following appropriate clinical evaluation.

Our team carefully assesses each patient to determine whether laser dentistry is appropriate for their specific treatment needs.

Precision technology. Gentle care. Better dental experiences.

Crowns, Bridges & Dentures

Restore your smile. Restore your confidence.

We offer customized restorative solutions to repair damaged teeth, replace missing teeth and restore comfortable chewing and a natural-looking smile.

Dental crowns

Dental crowns are customized restorations used to protect and strengthen weakened, fractured, heavily restored or root-treated teeth. They can also improve the shape, function and appearance of a tooth.

  • Porcelain or ceramic crowns
  • Zirconia crowns
  • Metal-ceramic crowns
  • Full-metal crowns, where clinically appropriate

Dental bridges

Dental bridges replace one or more missing teeth by using neighboring teeth or dental implants for support. They help restore chewing function, improve aesthetics and maintain the natural appearance of your smile.

  • Traditional fixed bridges
  • Ceramic or zirconia bridges
  • Implant-supported bridges

Dentures

Dentures are removable dental prostheses designed to replace multiple or all missing teeth. We offer customized options for improved comfort, appearance, speech and chewing ability.

  • Complete dentures
  • Partial dentures
  • Flexible dentures
  • Implant-supported dentures

Our team evaluates your oral health, functional needs and aesthetic preferences to recommend the restoration that best suits your individual requirements.

Strong teeth. Confident smiles. Comfortable function.
